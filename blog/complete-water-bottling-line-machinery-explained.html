The Complete Water Bottling Line: Machinery Explained Step by Step

20 Jul 2026 · 6 min read · by Bharat iON Systems

A modern packaged drinking water plant is an integrated line where each machine feeds the next. Here's how a complete bottling line works, step by step.

1. RO water treatment

The RO plant purifies raw water to drinking-water standards — the foundation of product quality.

2. PET bottle blowing

A PET blowing machine converts preforms into bottles in the sizes you need.

3. Rinsing, filling & capping

A 3-in-1 filling machine rinses empty bottles, fills purified water and applies caps hygienically.

4. Labelling

An automatic labelling machine applies your branded label neatly and at speed.

5. Batch coding

An inkjet coder prints batch number, MFG and EXP dates — required for compliance.

6. Shrink packaging

A shrink wrapping machine bundles bottles into secure, transport-ready packs.
