How to Start a Packaged Drinking Water Plant in India (2026 Guide)

18 Jun 2026 · 7 min read · by Bharat iON Systems

The demand for clean, packaged drinking water in India keeps growing across cities, towns and highways. Starting a packaged drinking water plant is a proven, scalable business — provided you set it up correctly. This guide walks you through the essentials.

1. Understand the business & market

Packaged drinking water is sold as pouches, cups, 250 ml–2 L bottles and 20 L jars. Decide your target formats and daily output first, because that determines the machinery capacity you need.

2. Licenses & registrations you need

  • BIS / ISI mark (IS 14543 for packaged drinking water) — mandatory.
  • FSSAI license for food safety.
  • Company/firm registration, GST, and a factory/trade license.
  • Local pollution board consent and a lab water test report.

3. Space & water source

A small plant typically needs 1,000–2,000 sq. ft. with a hygienic, well-ventilated production area, a bore well or reliable raw-water source, and adequate power.

4. Machinery required

A complete line usually includes:

  • Industrial RO water treatment plant — purifies raw water.
  • PET blowing machine — makes bottles from preforms (semi-automatic for smaller output).
  • Automatic filling & capping machine — rinses, fills and caps.
  • Sticker labelling machine and batch coding printer.
  • Shrink wrapping machine for transport-ready packs.

5. Approximate investment

Investment varies with capacity and level of automation — from a modest semi-automatic setup to a high-speed automatic line. Share your target output and we'll prepare a detailed, itemised quotation.

6. Step-by-step setup

  1. Finalise capacity & formats.
  2. Arrange space, water source and power.
  3. Apply for BIS & FSSAI licenses.
  4. Install RO plant + bottling line and test water quality.
  5. Start production, packaging and distribution.

Setting up correctly the first time saves cost and downtime later. As a manufacturer of the complete machinery range, we help you choose the right configuration and support you through installation and training.
