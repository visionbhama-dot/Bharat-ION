FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What machines does Bharat iON Systems manufacture?

We manufacture and supply complete water plant and bottle-packaging machinery, including RO water treatment plants, semi-automatic and fully automatic PET blowing machines, automatic bottle filling & capping machines, sticker labelling machines, shrink wrapping machines and inkjet batch coding printers.

Do you provide turnkey packaged drinking water plant setup?

Yes. We supply a complete turnkey line — from RO purification to blowing, filling, capping, labelling, coding and shrink packaging — along with installation, operator training and after-sales support.

What production capacities are available?

Our machines cover a wide range, from small units to high-speed lines up to 6,000 bottles per hour and RO plants from 250 to 10,000 litres per hour. We recommend the right configuration based on your target output.

Do you offer installation and after-sales support?

Yes, we provide installation, commissioning, operator training and prompt after-sales service and spare-parts support to keep your line running.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve customers across India and cater to distributors, dealers and OEM buyers. Contact us with your location and requirement for a tailored proposal.
