FAQ

Packaged Drinking Water Plant FAQs

How much does it cost to set up a packaged drinking water plant in India?

Cost depends on capacity, level of automation and the machines you choose. A small semi-automatic unit needs a modest investment, while a fully automatic high-speed line costs more. Share your target output and we will prepare a tailored budget and machine list.

What machines are needed for a mineral water plant?

A typical line includes an RO water treatment plant, a PET blowing machine, a rinsing-filling-capping machine, a labelling machine, a batch coding printer and a shrink wrapping machine, plus tanks, conveyors and utilities.

Do you provide turnkey installation and training?

Yes. We supply, install and commission the complete line, train your operators and provide after-sales service and spare parts.

How much space is required?

A small plant can start in roughly 1,000-2,000 sq ft, while larger automatic lines need more area for machines, storage and packaging. We help you plan the layout for your building.
